A record is flagged when the footage cannot show the work was done. The verdict decides what counts in the load totals. All three flags today trace back to edited or blocked footage, which fixed, well-placed station cameras should prevent.

One pose measurement — and why we don't score it

In G07's frame at 00:33.33, the left shoulder, hip and knee points give an angle of about 110°. That is a 2D projection, not a 3D joint angle: camera angle and point error limit what it means. The report uses pose points to decide whether the work is in view, not to grade it.

What this report does not claim
Bar speed, force or power Fatigue or readiness Injury risk or prediction Technique or form scores Tracking across the whole gym

Does not provide measured rep-count error, identity-switch rates or projected-angle accuracy. Loads: model identification and manual load input. Frames and pose overlays are anonymised.
